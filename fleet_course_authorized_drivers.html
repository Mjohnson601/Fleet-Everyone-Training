Section 1 of 5
Safe Operation of a Vehicle

These standards apply every time you drive on behalf of C Spire — regardless of what vehicle you're in. No exceptions, no gray areas.

  • 🚫Never drive while under the influence of alcohol, illegal drugs, or any medication that impairs your ability to drive safely.
  • 🪑Seat belts are mandatory for the driver and all passengers at all times.
  • 📵No hand-held phone use while driving. Hands-free only. No texting, emailing, or GPS programming while in motion.
  • 🏁Obey all speed limits, traffic signals, and signs. Drive defensively — not aggressively.
  • 😴Never drive when drowsy. During long trips, take a break every four hours. Do not exceed 10 hours of driving in any 24-hour period.
  • 🌧️When possible, avoid driving in dangerous conditions — flooded roads, heavy fog, icy conditions, or severe weather.
Real-World Example

You're driving to a customer site and get an urgent call from your manager. You're on the highway. The right move: let the call go to voicemail, or pull off safely and call back. Answering hand-held — even for 30 seconds — violates policy and could result in disciplinary action.

🚨
Radar Detectors Are ProhibitedUse of radar detectors in any C Spire or rental vehicle is strictly forbidden.

Distracted Driving Policy

C Spire's Distracted Driving Policy (Appendix A) is one of the most critical documents you acknowledged when joining the fleet program. Distractions cost lives — and violations can cost your job.

📋
Distractions Include:Talking on a cell phone without hands-free, texting, emailing, programming a GPS, eating, or using audio devices with headphones — anything that takes your eyes or attention off driving.

If you need to engage in any of these activities, you must first:

  • 🛑Pull over to a safe location on the shoulder.
  • 🎧Use a hands-free kit or Bluetooth device for calls.
  • ⚖️Follow all applicable state and local distracted driving laws.
🎧
Headphones Are Never Permitted While DrivingRegardless of what you're listening to, headphones while driving are prohibited — it's illegal in most states and prevents you from hearing sirens, horns, and warning signals.

Section 3 of 5
Collision & Damage Reporting

Accidents happen. What you do in the moments after matters enormously — for your safety, your colleagues, and the company. Know these steps before you ever need them.

📌
Critical Rule: Make No StatementsIf you are involved in a collision, be courteous — but make no statements regarding responsibility, fault, or liability to anyone at the scene (except law enforcement, in private).
  • 1️⃣Ensure your safety and the safety of any passengers. Call 911 immediately.
  • 2️⃣Contact C Spire Fleet Management or Safety at FleetAccidentReporting@cspire.com — on the date of occurrence.
  • 3️⃣Take photos of both vehicles, the full scene, traffic signals, signs, and road markings.
  • 4️⃣Collect the other driver's name, license number, insurance carrier, and policy number.
  • 5️⃣Get the police report number, officer name, and badge number if the full report isn't available on scene.
  • 6️⃣Report the incident to your supervisor by end of day — no exceptions.
🚫
Do Not Discuss the AccidentDo not discuss collision details with the other driver, adjusters, attorneys, media, or on social media — without authorization from C Spire Fleet, Safety, or Legal.
Scenario

After a minor fender-bender, the other driver is friendly and asks if you can "work this out" without filing a report. The right answer: decline politely, follow the steps above, and report to FleetAccidentReporting@cspire.com that same day. All accidents must be reported — no matter how minor.

Your Driving Record & Eligibility

C Spire reviews every authorized driver's Motor Vehicle Record (MVR) at least annually. Your ability to drive on behalf of the Company depends on maintaining an eligible record.

You will be disqualified from driving for C Spire if you have:

🚦More than 2 moving violations within a 3-year period (speeding, improper lane change, careless driving, etc.).
💥More than 1 at-fault collision within a 3-year period.
⚖️A combination of 2 moving violations + 1 at-fault collision within 3 years.
🍺Any major violation (DUI/DWI, reckless driving, hit and run, vehicular assault) within a 5-year period.
🪪A suspended or invalid driver's license — for any reason.
📢
Report Immediately — Even Personal ViolationsAny ticket, arrest, collision, or driving infraction — including those that occur during your personal time — must be reported to your manager immediately. A suspended license must also be reported to HR immediately.

Section 5 of 5
Alcohol & Drug Policy

This is one of the clearest policies in the Fleet Management document — and one of the most serious.

  • 🚫Under no circumstances may a driver operate a Company, rental, or personal vehicle on behalf of C Spire while impaired by alcohol, illegal drugs, or prescription medications that impair driving ability.
  • ⏱️If you are cited or convicted of driving under the influence — in any vehicle, including your personal car — you must report it to your supervisor within 24 hours.
  • ⚗️A drug and/or alcohol test may be administered after a collision, at Human Resources' discretion.
  • 🚨Violation of this policy will result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
